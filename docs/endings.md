# ENDINGS
### Subject — Ending Reference Document

> All 40 endings across 5 cases. Each case has 8 unique endings determined by the player's Empathy, Compliance, Sabotage, and Detection scores accumulated through notepad entries and fragment choices.

## Scoring System

| Score | Raised By | Lowered By |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| Empathy | Humanizing language in notepad, charitable interpretations, protecting subjects | Clinical detachment, framing subjects as data |
| Compliance | Efficient processing, accepting corporate framing, clean verdicts | Questioning the audit, redacting evidence |
| Sabotage | Redacting clues from the notepad | Filing complete reports |
| Detection | Each redaction — accumulates invisibly | Cannot be lowered once raised |

Endings are resolved at case close by reading the cumulative score state. High Empathy and High Compliance are mutually exclusive at the extremes — the player's notepad language pulls them toward one or the other across the case.
